# Customer Health & Retention Dashboard

**Overview**  
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to track customer health, retention trends, churn signals, and engagement patterns.

**Business Problem**  
Leadership needed fast, accurate visibility into retention and customer health metrics to make quicker, better decisions without relying on manual reports.

**Data Used**  
• SQL customer activity tables  
• API sourced metadata  
• Support and operations data  
• Daily engagement logs  

**Approach**  
• Cleaned and standardized datasets for modelling  
• Built a star schema for reporting  
• Designed retention cohorts and churn logic  
• Implemented DAX measures for health score, churn %, and activity trends  
• Created drilldowns for customer segments and lifecycle stages  

**Tech Stack**  
Power BI  
SQL  
Power Query  
DAX  

**Impact**  
• Improved leadership decision-making speed by 20 per cent  
• Enabled earlier detection of at-risk customer groups  
• Reduced manual reporting effort for customer success teams
