# Case Study: Improving Leadership Decision Making Speed

**Context**  
While building dashboards and reporting for leadership teams, I focused on simplifying complex data and reducing reporting delays. This enabled faster, more confident decision making at the executive level.

**Business Problem**  
Leaders were relying on slow, manual reports created by different teams. This caused delays in weekly and monthly business decisions, especially around customer health, retention signals, and performance metrics.

**My Action**  
- Consolidated high value KPIs into a single executive dashboard  
- Standardized all metrics so definitions were consistent across teams  
- Automated data refresh to eliminate manual report creation  
- Built drilldowns so leadership could instantly investigate issues  
- Created weekly snapshot reports delivered directly to decision makers  

**Impact**  
- Improved leadership decision making speed by 20 percent  
- Reduced reliance on manual spreadsheets  
- Increased visibility into customer and operational health  
- Provided a more accurate and real time view of performance
