# ETL Optimization Case Study

**Context**  
Across multiple roles, I worked on refactoring and improving ETL pipelines that powered daily, weekly, and monthly reporting for leadership and operations teams.

**Business Problem**  
The existing ETL workflows were slow, required manual intervention, and frequently caused delays in dashboards and reports. This created bottlenecks for teams relying on accurate daily metrics.

**My Contribution**  
- Cleaned and standardized the input datasets  
- Rewrote SQL queries to remove inefficiencies  
- Introduced incremental refresh logic  
- Added validation steps to identify missing or incorrect data  
- Automated ingestion schedules to eliminate manual refreshes  
- Documented the workflow so teams could maintain it independently  

**Impact**  
- Reduced manual data handling by nearly 30 per cent  
- Improved reliability and speed of daily refresh cycles  
- Reduced errors and improved accuracy of leadership level reporting  
- Helped teams access performance data faster and make quicker decisions
